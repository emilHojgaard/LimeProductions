Life

About

Based in Copenhagen, I'm a recent MSc graduate in Software Design from the IT University of Copenhagen, specialized in cybersecurity and web architecture / interaction design. My thesis — a RAG-based chatbot combining custom chunking, semantic and full-text search, and generative AI — received top marks.

The route here was unusual: a BA in Philosophy, a year in Paris, six months at sea with Maersk, and six months across Asia. It shows in the work — an eye for the human side of systems, and calm under pressure.
